Blood Sugar Premier™  Ingredients

Blood Sugar Premier™ is marketed as a dietary supplement designed to support healthy blood sugar levels and overall metabolic wellness. Its reported formula includes botanical ingredients and nutrients that have been studied for their roles in normal metabolic processes.

1

Berberine

Berberine is a plant-derived compound found in several plants, including barberry. It has been studied for its potential effects on glucose and lipid metabolism. Although some research is promising, its effects depend on dosage and individual circumstances, and it can interact with medications.
2

Turmeric Extract

Turmeric contains curcumin, a naturally occurring compound investigated for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. Researchers have explored its potential relationship with metabolic health, but more high-quality clinical studies are needed to establish its effectiveness for blood sugar management.
3

Black Pepper Extract

Black pepper extract contains piperine, a compound studied for its ability to influence the absorption of certain substances. It is sometimes paired with turmeric-derived curcumin in supplements. Piperine can also affect how the body processes some medicines, so potential interactions should be considered.
4

Fenugreek

Fenugreek is an herb whose seeds contain fiber and various plant compounds. It has a long history of culinary and traditional use and has been investigated for its possible effects on glucose metabolism. However, findings vary, and further research is needed to establish its effectiveness and appropriate use.
5

Gymnema Leaf Extract

Gymnema sylvestre is a climbing plant used in traditional Ayurvedic practices. Its leaves contain compounds being investigated for their potential effects on glucose metabolism and sweet-taste perception. Scientific evidence remains limited, and it should not replace prescribed treatment for diabetes.
6

American Ginseng

American ginseng contains naturally occurring compounds called ginsenosides. Researchers have investigated its possible relationship with glucose metabolism and other aspects of health. Its effects can vary according to the preparation and dose, and it may interact with certain medications.
7

Alpha-Lipoic Acid

Alpha-lipoic acid is involved in cellular energy metabolism and has antioxidant properties. It has been studied for its potential role in nerve health, including nerve-related complications associated with diabetes. However, it is not a substitute for glucose-lowering medication and may affect blood sugar levels.
8

Cinnamon Bark

Cinnamon is a widely used spice that contains naturally occurring plant compounds. It has been investigated for its potential effects on glucose metabolism, but research findings are mixed. High supplemental intake of certain cinnamon varieties may also pose liver-related risks.
9

Bitter Melon Extract

Bitter melon is a tropical fruit used in many traditional cuisines and herbal preparations. It contains compounds being investigated for their possible metabolic effects. However, clinical evidence is not sufficient to establish bitter melon as an effective diabetes treatment, and it may interact with blood sugar medication.
10

Chromium

Chromium is a trace mineral involved in normal nutrient metabolism. It has been studied for its potential relationship with insulin function and glucose regulation. However, evidence that chromium supplements consistently improve blood sugar control is limited, and supplementation should be discussed with a healthcare professional.
